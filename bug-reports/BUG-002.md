# BUG-002 – Product total not updated after quantity change

## Summary

The shopping cart does not update the product total when the user changes the item quantity.

## Environment

* OS: Windows 11
* Browser: Google Chrome
* Browser Version: Latest available version
* Environment: Test Environment

## Preconditions

* The user is logged in.
* At least one product is available in the shopping cart.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Log in to the application.
2. Add a product to the shopping cart.
3. Open the shopping cart.
4. Change the product quantity from 1 to 3.
5. Observe the displayed product total.

## Test Data

* Product price: €20
* Initial quantity: 1
* Updated quantity: 3

## Expected Result

The product total should be updated to €60.

## Actual Result

The product total remains €20 after changing the quantity to 3.

## Severity

**Major**

## Priority

**High**

## Status

**Open**

## Impact

Users may be charged an incorrect amount or receive misleading pricing information during the checkout process.

## Recommendation

Review the cart calculation logic and ensure that price calculations are triggered whenever the product quantity changes.
